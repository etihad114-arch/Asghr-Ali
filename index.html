FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work completely online?

Yes. CAD, Civil 3D, GIS, survey-data processing, BOQ, documentation and many design-support services can be handled remotely.

What files can I send?

DWG, DXF, PDF, Excel, CSV, survey coordinates, photographs and ZIP packages can be used depending on the service.

How quickly can work be delivered?

Small tasks may take 1–2 working days; larger engineering packages require an agreed schedule after reviewing the scope and information.

Do you provide construction-site services?

Yes. Site inspection, setting out, measurements, drawing checks, contractor coordination, QA/QC and construction support are available where location and project arrangements permit.

Can you prepare complete project documentation?

Yes. Depending on scope, deliverables can include CAD drawings, BOQ, BBS, reports, inspection records, as-built drawings and handover documentation.

Do you support international clients?

Online engineering and documentation services can be provided internationally, subject to project requirements, applicable standards and agreed scope.
